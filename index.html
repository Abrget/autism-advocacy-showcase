Join Us

Our Activities

Discover a range of engaging activities designed to support, educate, and empower individuals across the autism spectrum.

Every Saturday

Sensory-Friendly Workshops

Interactive workshops designed for individuals with sensory sensitivities, providing a safe and comfortable environment to learn and engage.

Tuesdays & Thursdays

Art Therapy Sessions

Creative expression sessions that help individuals on the spectrum communicate emotions and develop fine motor skills through various art forms.

First Monday of each month

Family Support Groups

Regular meetings where families can share experiences, challenges, and solutions while building a supportive community network.

Wednesdays

Life Skills Training

Practical sessions focused on developing essential daily living and social skills for independent living and community integration.

Quarterly Events

Autism Awareness Walks

Community events that raise awareness about autism while promoting inclusion and celebrating neurodiversity through organized walks.
